Privacy Policy

Little Muslim Learners · Published by ATM Digital Solutions & Services · Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Little Muslim Learners app uses, and why. The app is made for children, and we keep the information it uses to a minimum.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for a name, email address, phone number, school or date of birth.
  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not track users across other apps or websites.

2. Information stored on the device

The app saves some settings and progress on the phone or tablet only, such as:

  • language, theme and text size
  • stars and lesson progress
  • Quran bookmarks and the last surah read
  • tasbih counts and reminder settings
  • prayer location (latitude and longitude, only after you tap "Use my location") and Adhan reminder settings
  • the daily checklist and badges

This information stays on the device. It is removed when the app is uninstalled or when the browser's site data is cleared.

3. Anonymous sign-in

The app uses Google Firebase to start an anonymous session. Firebase gives the app a random identifier that is not linked to any name or account. It is used only to keep the app's connection to its online services secure.

4. Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders (Ayatul Kursi, Istighfar) or Adhan reminders for prayer times, the app shows notifications and may play a sound on the device. Your location is used only to calculate prayer times on your device; it is not sent to us. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app, or in your phone's notification settings.

5. Online content

Some Quran text, reciter lists, Hadith text and audio are loaded from outside services, including quranenc.com, mp3quran.net, cdn.jsdelivr.net and opendua.org. When the app loads this content, those services can see the internet address (IP address) of the device, as their own policies describe. We do not send them any other personal information.

6. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians can review and delete the app's stored information by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

7. Security

The settings and progress listed in section 2 stay on the device. The connections to online services use secure HTTPS links.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
